How it starts

The 18-Hole Qualifier

All 36 teams take the course at once. Every one of the 18 holes is shared by two 4-man teams playing the full round together — eight golfers per hole, every hole occupied from the first tee shot to the final putt.

Format is a best-ball scramble. When the round is complete, the leaderboard is set and the top 10 teams are notified immediately. There is no break. The Championship Derby starts now.

When the qualifier ends

The Championship Derby

The moment the 18-hole qualifier wraps, the top 10 teams walk straight to Hole 1. No break. The Championship Derby is single-elimination alternate shot — every hole, one team goes home.

10

Teams Enter the Derby

The top 10 qualifiers step to the 1st tee together. The rest of the field watches.

Alt

Alternate Shot, Every Hole

No scramble, no best ball. Partners alternate shots on the same ball — pure golf, full pressure.

−1

Highest Score Eliminated

After every hole, the team with the worst score is out. Single elimination, no second chances.

9

Holes Until a Champion

Nine holes of sudden death. One team standing at the end takes home the top prize.
